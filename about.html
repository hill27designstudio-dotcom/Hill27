The studio

Design that survives contact with a factory floor

Hill27 Design Studio Pvt Ltd partners with fashion brands, startups, and manufacturers to turn creative ideas into market-ready apparel and lifestyle products. We're based in Mettupalayam, on the edge of Coimbatore — a region with denim and textile manufacturing in its bones — and we work close to the factories our designs eventually pass through.

A lot of design work stops at the sketch or the mood board. Ours doesn't. Every piece we design is developed alongside a tech pack, a fabric plan, and a realistic path to production, so what looks good on paper still looks good coming off the line.

We take on denim development, brand identity, and full collections — but the throughline is the same on every project: design decisions made with manufacturing in the room, not bolted on afterward.

How we work

Three stages, always in order

We don't jump to tech packs before the design direction is settled, even under deadline.

STAGE / 01

Design

Concept, silhouette, fabric and trend direction — grounded in what the brand and market actually need.

STAGE / 02

Develop

STAGE / 03

Produce

Hands-on support through sampling and manufacturing, so nothing gets lost in translation on the floor.
